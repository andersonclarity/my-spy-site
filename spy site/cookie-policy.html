How We Use the Data Collected

We only collect marketing data that is used for promotional purposes. Certain personal information, including names, addresses, telephone number, and email address may also be collected on the checkout page. But we do not share the latter with third parties.

Marketing

We will only send you marketing information if you opt-in to receive such communication. You can also choose to opt-out of such communication at any time. Every marketing email that we send you will have instructions on how to opt-out.

Cookies and Log Files

Cookie technology is a part of our website’s statistical reporting. The cookie is a small file that is stored on your computer to track your usage of our website and it is meant to personalize your user experience. While the cookie itself doesn’t contain any information, it can help us provide a more user-friendly experience every time you visit our website. The information we gather includes the pages you visit, the products you download, the internet provider’s domain, country of origin, and the web addresses of the websites you visit before and after our website.

We also use the standard log files. These can help us determine the number of people who visit our website to help us evaluate our website’s capacity and improve when necessary. All pages on our website are intended to be as user-friendly as possible and we may use third-party tools like Google Analytics to help measure the effectiveness of your marketing strategies as well as our website’s efficiency and speed.

For example, we may provide your users’ data to service providers who perform various services on our behalf. These service providers are however not allowed to use the information we provide for their own marketing purposes. We may also disclose your personal information if required by law enforcement or as part of legal processes.

Security

The privacy and security of your data is very important to us. We therefore take all the necessary precautions to ensure the safety of any personal information you may enter on our website against destruction, loss, or unauthorized access.
