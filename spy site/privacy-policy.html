Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the operations of the Discreetspy website (“Our Website”) and our company. It explains how we collect, use, and protect user data, and outlines your rights and responsibilities when using our services.

Consent and Data Collection from Target Devices

Discreetspy collects data from a target device only after receiving explicit consent from the user who sets up the application. By completing the setup, users confirm that they are legally authorized to monitor the device in accordance with applicable laws and have obtained all necessary consents from the device owner—or are the legal guardian of a minor. No data is collected until consent is confirmed. This consent is securely logged for compliance purposes. Our goal is to ensure transparency and operate strictly within legal boundaries for responsible monitoring.

Data Collection and Use

You may browse our website without submitting personal information. However, when you purchase our software and set it up on a device, we may collect personal data including, but not limited to, your name, email address, billing address, and payment details. Some of this information may be collected by third-party payment processors, and not shared with us.

Following a successful transaction, we will send you a welcome email with login credentials to access your dashboard. We may also request additional information to enable certain features or improve your experience. Declining to provide such data may limit your use of some services.

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties without your explicit consent. All data entered on our website is secured using password protection and encryption. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your password, especially on shared devices.

We may occasionally send promotional emails related to our services or trusted partners. These emails will always come from us. You can opt out at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions in each message.

Additional Data Use and Third-Party Links

We may share non-identifiable, aggregated demographic information (such as age, education level, or income bracket) with third parties for analytical or research purposes.

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. These sites operate independently and have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their content or data practices, and we encourage you to review their policies before sharing personal information.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. This includes Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) encryption for all transactions and account activity. Personal data is encrypted, stored securely, and only accessible to authorized personnel. Data is decrypted only upon verified user login.

Legal Compliance and Disclosures

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal processes such as subpoenas or court orders. In the event of a business transfer (e.g., merger or acquisition), your personal data may be transferred as part of the transaction, in accordance with applicable legal standards.

User Rights

You have the right to access, update, modify, or delete your personal information at any time. You may also decline future changes to this Privacy Policy or discontinue the use of our services. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under laws such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including data portability and the right to restrict processing.

Our services are intended only for individuals aged 18 and above. By purchasing or using our software, you confirm that you meet this age requirement. If the software is used for parental control purposes, you must confirm that you are the legal guardian of the monitored minor and provide the necessary consent. Data collected from minors is never shared with third parties.

This Privacy Policy is governed by applicable local laws as well as relevant international data protection regulations.
